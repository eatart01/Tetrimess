⚔️ Versus

Goal

Take turns dropping blocks. 11 dropped off the edge = you lose.

"Safe In" Countdown

Blocks get a few seconds to settle before your turn ends.

⚠️ A block can fall late. If it falls after the turn has passed, it counts for whoever's turn it is then — not who dropped it!
Page 3 / 3
🏆 Topple

Goal

Knock blocks off to score. First to 35 points wins.

Scoring

Score = cells that fall, not blocks.

3-Strike Timer

  • 1st time-out: free.
  • 2nd: −1 point.
  • 3rd: you lose.
⚠️ Same as Versus — a late fall counts for whoever's turn it is then, not who caused it!
